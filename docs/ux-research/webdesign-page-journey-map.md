## Key Moments of Truth

### ⚡ Moment 1: First Impression (0-3 seconds)
**Current**: Hero with technical focus  
**Ideal**: Benefit-focused headline + price + trust badge  
**Impact**: HIGH - Determines if user continues

### 💰 Moment 2: Price Discovery (10-30 seconds)
**Current**: Requires scrolling to pricing section  
**Ideal**: Price visible in hero or trust bar  
**Impact**: HIGH - Major decision factor

### 🤝 Moment 3: Trust Validation (30-60 seconds)
**Current**: Testimonials exist but not prominent  
**Ideal**: Trust bar + specific testimonials + case studies  
**Impact**: HIGH - Reduces skepticism

### 📝 Moment 4: Contact Decision (2-5 minutes)
**Current**: Form may feel like commitment  
**Ideal**: Multiple low-commitment options + clear next steps  
**Impact**: CRITICAL - Determines conversion

## Friction Points & Solutions

### Friction Point 1: "Is this for me?"
**Location**: Hero section  
**Solution**: Add qualification statement: "Perfect for Swiss SMEs with 10-50 employees looking to grow online"

### Friction Point 2: "What's the real cost?"
**Location**: Pricing section  
**Solution**: Add comparison table showing total cost vs. alternatives, highlight "all-inclusive"

### Friction Point 3: "Can I trust them?"
**Location**: Throughout page  
**Solution**: Add trust bar, specific testimonials with results, case studies, Swiss quality badges

### Friction Point 4: "What if I'm not satisfied?"
**Location**: FAQ / Pricing  
**Solution**: Add satisfaction guarantee, free consultation, no-obligation messaging

### Friction Point 5: "Contacting feels like commitment"
**Location**: Contact form  
**Solution**: Add low-commitment options (chat, calendar booking), show "what happens next"
