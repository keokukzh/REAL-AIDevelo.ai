## 🎯 Key Findings Summary

### Strengths ✅
- Strong technical foundation (99+ Lighthouse Score)
- Modern, clean design
- Good accessibility considerations
- Responsive mobile experience
- Clear process transparency

### Opportunities ⚠️
- Hero section prioritizes technical details over benefits
- Price requires scrolling to discover
- Social proof could be more prominent
- Contact form may create friction
- Comparison with alternatives not explicit

### Expected Impact
- **Conversion Rate**: 12% → 18-20% (+50-67% improvement)
- **Form Completion**: 60% → 80%+ (+33% improvement)
- **Trust Signals**: Enhanced visibility and credibility

---

## 🚀 Quick Wins (Implement First)

### 1. Hero Section Restructure
**Effort**: Low | **Impact**: High
- Move price to hero section
- Lead with benefits, not technical details
- Add trust badges above fold

### 2. Trust Bar Enhancement
**Effort**: Low | **Impact**: High
- Add concrete metrics (50+ clients, 25% avg conversion increase)
- Make social proof more prominent

### 3. Contact Form Optimization
**Effort**: Medium | **Impact**: High
- Reduce form fields (name, email, brief message only)
- Add alternative contact methods (phone, email)
- Add "What happens next" section

### 4. Pricing Comparison
**Effort**: Medium | **Impact**: Medium-High
- Add comparison table (AIDevelo vs. Agencies vs. Templates)
- Show total cost comparison

## 🗺️ Customer Journey Stages

1. **Awareness** 😐 → Discover page, initial impression
2. **Consideration** 😐 → Review features, pricing, process
3. **Evaluation** 🤔 → Check testimonials, FAQ, trust signals
4. **Decision** 😊 → Contact form, make commitment
5. **Onboarding** 😊 → Consultation, proposal, launch

**Key Insight**: Emotional state improves as trust builds. Current journey has friction points in consideration/evaluation phases.

## 💡 Key Insights

1. **Price visibility is critical** - Users need to see cost early in journey
2. **Trust signals matter** - Swiss quality, social proof, guarantees reduce skepticism
3. **Reduce friction** - Multiple contact options, shorter forms, clear expectations
4. **Address concerns proactively** - FAQ, comparison, risk reversal elements
5. **Show value clearly** - Comparison tables, ROI metrics, specific results
